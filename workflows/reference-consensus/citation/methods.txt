A reference-guided consensus of ${config.inputs.selected_isolates} haploid isolates was built against the backbone assembly (${input.backbone}) with the ${workflow.id} workflow (version ${workflow.version}) of GenoPilot ${genopilot.version}, run with Snakemake ${tool.snakemake}. For every read pair, fastp ${tool.fastp} detected and trimmed adapters, trimmed poly-G tails of at least 10 bases, and removed reads shorter than 30 bp, without quality filtering. Reads were aligned with bwa mem ${tool.bwa} with a fixed batch size and read groups derived from the Illumina read names; the alignments of each isolate were merged and duplicates marked per library with samtools ${tool.samtools}. Every covered position was called with bcftools ${tool.bcftools} mpileup and call (multiallelic caller, ploidy ${config.calling.ploidy}), counting reads with a mapping quality of at least ${config.calling.min_mapping_quality} and bases with a quality of at least ${config.calling.min_base_quality}, skipping unmapped, secondary, QC-failed, and duplicate reads, with a maximum depth of 10000 and indel candidates supported by at least 2 reads and 5% of the reads; variants were normalized with bcftools norm. A position of an isolate was callable when it had at least ${config.calling.min_depth} reads and one allele reached a fraction of ${config.calling.min_allele_fraction} of them. At every backbone position, the callable isolates voted by ${config.consensus.voting_method}, and ${config.consensus.include_backbone_vote}.${config.consensus.min_callable_isolates} Unresolved SNPs, with a tie or without a majority, were ${config.consensus.unresolved_snp}. The support and consensus tables were compressed and indexed with bgzip and tabix from HTSlib ${tool.htslib}.
