A reference-guided consensus of ${config.inputs.selected_isolates} haploid isolates was built against the backbone assembly (${input.backbone}) with the ${workflow.id} workflow (version ${workflow.version}) of GenoPilot ${genopilot.version}, run with Snakemake ${tool.snakemake}. For input file groups not already processed by the provider, fastp ${tool.fastp} detected paired-end adapters and removed reads shorter than 30 bp without read-quality filtering; poly-G trimming followed the per-read-set effective settings recorded in the isolate snapshot and provenance. Provider-processed groups bypassed fastp unchanged. Reads were aligned with bwa mem ${tool.bwa} with a fixed batch size and generated read-group IDs, user-confirmed library identities, and optional sequencing metadata derived from original Illumina headers; the alignments of each isolate were merged and duplicates marked per library with samtools ${tool.samtools}. Every covered position was called with bcftools ${tool.bcftools} mpileup and call (multiallelic caller, ploidy ${config.calling.ploidy}), counting reads with a mapping quality of at least ${config.calling.min_mapping_quality} and bases with a quality of at least ${config.calling.min_base_quality}, skipping unmapped, secondary, QC-failed, and duplicate reads, with a maximum depth of 10000 and indel candidates supported by at least 2 reads and 5% of the reads; variants were normalized with bcftools norm. A position of an isolate was callable when it had at least ${config.calling.min_depth} reads and one allele reached a fraction of ${config.calling.min_allele_fraction} of them. At every backbone position, the callable isolates voted by ${config.consensus.voting_method}, and ${config.consensus.include_backbone_vote}.${config.consensus.min_callable_isolates} Unresolved SNPs, with a tie or without a majority, were ${config.consensus.unresolved_snp}. The support and consensus tables were compressed and indexed with bgzip and tabix from HTSlib ${tool.htslib}.
